# MeterMatch Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 30 October 2026  
**Application:** MeterMatch (`de.alphaomegatechnology.meterrecognition`)  
**Provider:** Alpha-Omega Technology GmbH & Co. KG, Flinsberger Straße 27, 37308 Schimberg, Germany  
**Privacy contact:** info@ao-t.de

## 1. Scope
This Privacy Policy describes how MeterMatch processes information when you use its local meter recognition, cloud-based recognition, retrofit compatibility, and AI Retrofit Search features. MeterMatch is intended to help identify utility meters and investigate compatible retrofit equipment. AI-assisted results are informational and do not replace technical verification before installation.

MeterMatch has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not access your location, contacts, or device identifiers.

## 2. Information processed
**Photos you select or capture.** The app accesses a meter image when you use its camera or image-selection features. A photo may include meter labels, serial numbers, surroundings, or other information visible in the image. Please avoid including people or unnecessary personal information. Before recognition, the app creates a separate copy of the photo, limited to 2048 pixels on its longest side, from which embedded file metadata (such as EXIF data, including any GPS location) is removed. The original photo on your device is not changed. Visible content in the image is not removed.

**Meter details.** Recognition and retrofit functions may process the meter manufacturer, model, type, and related technical attributes.

**Technical request information.** Each cloud request carries a technical request identifier generated by the app from the time of the request. To protect the service against excessive use, the backend counts requests per minute, hour, and day; these counters contain no information about you or your device and are deleted after three days. As with any internet service, the hosting infrastructure that receives a request necessarily processes the IP address of your connection in order to deliver the response. The MeterMatch request handling itself does not store IP addresses.

## 3. Local recognition and offline retrofit checks
Local Recognition runs the recognition model on your device, and the offline retrofit database is part of the app. Neither uploads the selected meter photo or sends any other data. They also work without an internet connection.

MeterMatch only uses the network when you choose Cloud Recognition (Section 4), when you switch on AI Retrofit Search (Section 5), or when you open a product or source link, which opens in your external web browser. Websites opened in this way are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 4. Cloud Recognition
Cloud Recognition is initiated when you choose that feature. The prepared photo (see Section 2) is transmitted over HTTPS to the MeterMatch backend, which forwards it to AI service providers for analysis. The currently integrated providers are OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), and Google (Gemini). Each provider analyses the image independently and returns potential meter identification results. The backend then compares the identified meter against the MeterMatch retrofit compatibility database, which is hosted by our database provider in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

The providers' own processing, security, and retention of the data they receive are governed by their respective service terms. We have not yet verified the specific processing locations, retention periods, or contractual arrangements that apply to each provider; see Section 8.

## 5. AI Retrofit Search
If you switch on AI Retrofit Search and the MeterMatch database contains no verified retrofit for the identified meter, the app sends the meter's identity information (manufacturer, model family, model, variant, and utility type) to the MeterMatch backend, which uses OpenAI with web search to look for documented retrofit compatibility. The meter photo is not included in this request. The service returns a compatible retrofit product only when manufacturer documentation verifies it; otherwise it states that no compatible retrofit could be verified.

## 6. Purposes and legal bases of processing
Information is processed to provide the features you request; identify meters; assess retrofit compatibility; return relevant product information; protect the service against misuse; and diagnose technical failures. We do not use meter images or meter details for advertising, and we do not sell them.

The legal bases under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are:
- **Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the service you request):** processing the meter photo for Cloud Recognition and the meter details for AI Retrofit Search, which happens only when you use these features, including the transmission to the AI service providers named in Sections 4 and 5.
- **Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests):** limiting the number of requests to protect the service against misuse, processing the IP address of your connection to deliver responses, and keeping records of failed requests for up to 28 days to diagnose technical failures. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of MeterMatch. You may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR); see Section 10.

## 7. Storage and retention
- **Successful requests:** the backend (n8n) does not save a record of successful Cloud Recognition or AI Retrofit Search requests. Neither the photo nor the result is kept after the response has been sent.
- **Failed requests:** when a request fails, the backend keeps the execution record so that the failure can be diagnosed. For Cloud Recognition, this record can include the submitted photo. The backend deletes such records automatically after at most **28 days**.
- **Request counters:** the counters described in Section 2 contain no personal data and are deleted after three days.
- **On your device:** MeterMatch does not keep a history of photos or results. Your camera or gallery app may keep the original photo according to its own settings.
- **AI providers:** data sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google is retained by those providers according to their own service terms.

## 8. Disclosure to third parties
When Cloud Recognition is used, meter photos are transmitted to the providers named in Section 4. When AI Retrofit Search is used, meter identity data is transmitted to OpenAI. We do not disclose this information to any other third party for their own purposes, and we do not sell it.

These providers may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. We have not yet verified the contractual roles (for example, whether each provider acts as a data processor on our behalf), the data processing agreements in place, or the safeguards for international transfers. We will update this policy once that review is complete.

## 9. Security
MeterMatch transmits data to its backend only over HTTPS. The access credentials for the AI services are held on the backend and are not contained in the app. The backend accepts only JPEG and PNG images of up to 5 MB and limits the number of requests it processes. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Your choices and rights
You can use Local Recognition and offline retrofit checks without choosing Cloud Recognition. AI Retrofit Search is off by default and is used only when you switch it on. You may decline to submit an image to cloud services.

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to certain processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In Germany, the authority responsible for Alpha-Omega Technology is the Thüringer Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (TLfDI).

To request information or deletion, contact info@ao-t.de. Because MeterMatch has no user accounts, we can only locate a stored record if you give us details such as the date and approximate time of the request. In most cases no record exists: successful requests are not stored, and records of failed requests are deleted automatically after at most 28 days.

## 11. Children
MeterMatch is designed for professional use and is intended for users aged 16 and over. It is not directed to children.

## 12. Changes to this policy
